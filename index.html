Deckworth

Scan your cards. Know what they're worth.

Deckworth is a trading card scanner for Android. Hold a Yu-Gi-Oh! card up to the camera and it reads the set code and shows the current market price.

  • Prices by rarity, edition and condition
  • Hands-free scanning with a card stand
  • Collection lists, value history and export (Pro and Ultimate)

Coming soon to Google Play.

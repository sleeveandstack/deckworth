Deckworth Privacy Policy

Deckworth is made by Sleeve & Stack (Dalten Otten). Effective date: October 27, 2026.

This policy explains what information the Deckworth app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the camera is read on your phone, your card lists stay on your phone, we don't show ads, and we never sell your information.

1. Information the app uses

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Camera To read the set code and name printed on a card so the app can identify it. Photos are processed on your phone and deleted right after they're read. They are not uploaded or saved.
Google account (name, email address, account ID) To sign you in, keep your plan (Free, Pro or Ultimate) with your account, and apply daily usage limits. Sent to our server (hosted by Supabase) when you sign in with Google.
Card set codes and names you look up (for example, DOOD-EN010) To get current card prices, and a picture of the card when you open it in the card viewer. Sent to our server, which looks up prices from JustTCG and card pictures from a public card database (YGOPRODeck). Those services receive only the card or set being looked up, not who you are.
Plan and usage counts (how many scans, lookups and new sets you used today) To run the Free, Pro and Ultimate plans and prevent abuse. Stored on our server with your account.
Your card lists, settings and value history So your collection is there next time you open the app. Stored only on your phone. We don't receive them.
Purchases (if you buy a plan) To unlock your plan on your account. Handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of your plan, never your payment card details.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't use your information to build a profile of you.
  • We don't upload your camera photos or your card lists.

3. Services we use

  • Google Sign-In: to sign you in with your Google account.
  • Supabase: hosts our server, sign-in system and database.
  • JustTCG: provides card price data. It receives card and set codes from our server, not your personal information.
  • YGOPRODeck: provides card pictures. Our server downloads each picture once and stores it; your information is never sent to it.
  • Google ML Kit: reads text from the camera on your phone, without sending images to us.
  • Google Play: app distribution and, if you buy a plan, payments.

Each of these services handles information under its own privacy policy.

4. Sharing your list

If you use Export or Share, the app creates a file or text and hands it to the app you choose (for example, Gmail or Google Drive). It goes only where you send it.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information and usage counts while your account exists. Card price data is shared by all users and isn't linked to you. If you delete your account, we delete your account information and usage records.

6. Your choices

  • Camera: you can turn off camera permission in your phone's settings. You can still add cards by hand.
  • Sign out anytime from Settings in the app. Your lists stay on your phone.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This removes your account, plan and usage records from our server right away. You can also email us at the address below from your Google account's email and we'll delete it within 30 days. If you pay for a plan, also cancel it in Google Play.
  • Delete data on your phone: clear a list in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone.

7. Security

Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted to our server. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Deckworth isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or the store listing.

Delete your Deckworth account

Deckworth is made by Sleeve & Stack. This page explains how to delete your Deckworth account and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Deckworth and tap the gear icon (⚙) to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Tap again to delete account to confirm.

Your account is deleted from our server right away.

Or ask us by email

If you can't use the app, email daltenotten@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Deckworth account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Deckworth sign-in (your Google account's email address and account ID as stored by Deckworth)
  • Your plan (Free, Pro or Ultimate)
  • Your usage records (daily scan, lookup and new-set counts)

What is kept

  • Card lists on your phone: these were never stored on our server. They stay on your phone until you clear them in the app or uninstall Deckworth.
  • Card prices and card pictures: shared by all users and not linked to you.
  • Nothing about you is kept after deletion. There is no extra retention period.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid plan. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Deckworth → Cancel.
